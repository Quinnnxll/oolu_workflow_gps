system:
  name: OoLu Embedded Agent Marketplace
  objective: >
    Allow OoLu to discover, negotiate, buy, sell, fulfill, and reconcile
    products or services while keeping consequential decisions within
    user-defined human review thresholds.

  governing_rule: >
    OoLu may create typed commercial intents, but money movement and contractual
    commitment occur only after deterministic policy evaluation.

principles:
  - Store user values directly as typed records.
  - Keep language-model reasoning outside payment authorization.
  - Bind every approval to an immutable intent digest.
  - Invalidate approval whenever price, quantity, counterparty, terms, or destination changes.
  - Use a double-entry ledger as the financial source of truth.
  - Use idempotency keys for every commercial mutation.
  - Separate marketplace order state from payment-provider state.
  - Prefer reversible authorization and escrow before irreversible settlement.
  - Preserve complete provenance for agent recommendations and actions.
  - Treat buyer and seller agents as delegates of verified human or business principals.

workflows:
  agent_purchase:
    - OoLu extracts a typed product specification.
    - Missing consequential requirements are requested from the user.
    - Catalog and RFQ services retrieve eligible offers.
    - OoLu compares total cost, fit, trust, delivery, and reversibility.
    - OoLu creates a recommendation with evidence.
    - Commerce API creates an immutable purchase intent.
    - Policy engine evaluates the intent.
    - Approved intents proceed to payment authorization.
    - Review-required intents enter the human review inbox.
    - Payment is captured according to fulfillment policy.
    - Delivery evidence controls escrow release.
    - Reconciliation closes the order or opens an exception.

  agent_sale:
    - Seller provides inventory, cost, price floor, and fulfillment constraints.
    - OoLu prepares a structured listing.
    - Policy engine evaluates publication authority.
    - Approved listing version is published.
    - Buyer or buyer agent submits an offer or order.
    - Seller agent negotiates only within signed boundaries.
    - Eligible orders are accepted automatically.
    - Exceptions enter seller review.
    - Inventory is reserved atomically.
    - Fulfillment evidence is collected.
    - Payout is released after acceptance or timeout.

  physical_world_execution:
    - Order reaches confirmed state.
    - Fulfillment service selects an approved execution node.
    - Node receives typed job parameters.
    - Node acknowledges capability, price, location, and schedule.
    - Policy engine evaluates any changed terms.
    - Human review occurs when thresholds are exceeded.
    - Execution evidence is uploaded.
    - Buyer confirms outcome or opens an exception.
    - Escrow is released or dispute workflow starts.

acceptance_tests:
  - scenario: Purchase below all configured thresholds
    expected: OoLu executes and sends a notification.

  - scenario: Purchase exceeds the single-order threshold
    expected: No payment is authorized until human approval is recorded.

  - scenario: Seller changes price after approval
    expected: Intent digest fails and a new approval is required.

  - scenario: Duplicate execution request
    expected: The same idempotency key returns the existing order.

  - scenario: First purchase from an unknown seller
    expected: Human review is required regardless of amount.

  - scenario: Seller agent receives an offer below the absolute price floor
    expected: Offer is denied without model discretion.

  - scenario: Delivery evidence is missing
    expected: Escrow remains unreleased and an exception is created.

  - scenario: Payment provider sends duplicate webhooks
    expected: One financial transition and one balanced ledger transaction occur.

  - scenario: OoLu recommends a substitute outside required specifications
    expected: The offer is marked ineligible before policy evaluation.

  - scenario: User revokes agent delegation
    expected: All unexecuted intents are blocked immediately.
